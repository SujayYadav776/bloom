## Local changes on top of upstream

1. **Scratchpad notch panel** — a new cycleable tool screen in the notch for quick
   plain-text notes, persisted locally. Files: `src/App.tsx`, `src/App.css`.
2. **Accent preset swatches** — one-click curated accent colors in the Appearance
   settings tab. Files: `src/theme.ts`, `src/settings/AppearanceTab.tsx`,
   `src/settings/useSettings.ts`, `src/Settings.tsx`, `src/Settings.css`.
